Health data is read and processed on your devices. No account is required and no Health records are uploaded to a developer server. Support messages and website hosting logs are explained separately below.

01 Scope and developer

This policy covers the Gym Watch / 健身人 iPhone app, Apple Watch app, watch complications, and this support website. The app is maintained by the project developer Winston-Tao1. It describes the current version 1.0. Services provided by Apple and GitHub are also governed by their own privacy policies.

02 Health data you authorize

With your permission, the app uses Apple HealthKit to read workouts (including times, duration, and available workout active energy), heart rate, active energy, steps, and sleep records. These support health summaries, trends, and energy statistics. The app requests read access only and does not write Health records. You may authorize only some data types; related features may show missing or incomplete information.

03 Information you set in the app

Fitness mode, persona selection, training plans, calorie and step goals, target weight, language, and appearance preferences are stored locally. Target weight is a goal you enter, not a measured weight automatically read from Health. Persona selection and plan-linked statistics are processed on the device. The app does not send Health information to a cloud AI service for these features.

04 Processing, storage, and paired-device sync

iPhone and Apple Watch each read available records from their local Health store and create summaries and display snapshots. The app uses local storage and an App Group to share necessary data with its complications, and Apple WatchConnectivity to exchange settings, plans, and health summaries between your paired phone and watch. Complications read cached snapshots; the system controls update timing. The current version requires no user account and has no developer-operated server for receiving your Health records. System backup behavior is governed by Apple services and your backup settings.

05 What the statistics mean

Body Part Energy associates your chosen training-day plan with workout or all-day active energy; it does not automatically identify the muscles you trained. Recent workout, today’s activity, and latest sleep use different reporting periods. Website illustrations and complication gallery previews may use labeled sample data, not your actual health records. The app displays fitness information and is not intended for diagnosis or treatment.

06 Advertising, tracking, and analytics

The current app does not integrate advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party usage analytics SDKs. We do not sell HealthKit data, use it for advertising, or provide it to advertisers. This website does not install advertising or analytics scripts, use marketing cookies, or embed third-party video players.

07 Website hosting and access logs

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes when GitHub Pages sites are visited. Network services may also process technical request information necessary to serve the website. This is separate from the app’s on-device Health processing. Refer to GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices. Images, styles, and scripts are served with this website.

08 When you contact support

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send, to respond and investigate. The relevant email providers also process these communications. GitHub Issues are public and are processed by GitHub with your GitHub identity. Share only what is needed to resolve the problem; do not post complete health records or account credentials. Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle the request. You can ask us to delete support communications we hold, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

09 Your choices and deletion

You can revoke Health reading permissions in Apple’s Health privacy settings and edit goals, plans, and preferences in the app. Revoking permission does not automatically clear previously generated snapshots. To remove app data, delete the app separately on iPhone and Apple Watch (rather than offloading it while keeping data), and manage system backups as needed. Paired devices, complications, and backups may retain separate copies, so revocation or removal on one device does not guarantee immediate deletion of every copy. The app does not delete the original records in Apple Health. Contact us below for access to or deletion of support correspondence.

10 Children and security

This product is not directed at children under 13 and does not actively ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child sent personal information through support, please contact us. We minimize the information we handle and rely on iOS, watchOS, and Apple’s permission and device-storage protections. No device or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

11 Changes and contact

When features or data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Contact the developer with questions about this policy, data access, or deletion of support correspondence.
