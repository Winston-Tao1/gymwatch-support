Good questions. Clear answers.

How do I install the Apple Watch app?+

Install Gym Watch on your paired iPhone, then open the Watch app on iPhone. Find Gym Watch in the available apps list under My Watch and choose Install. Keep Bluetooth and network connectivity available, and check that both devices run compatible system versions.

How do I add a complication to my watch face?+

Touch and hold your Apple Watch face, choose Edit, and swipe to Complications. Select a supported circular or rectangular position and choose a Gym Watch complication. Available slots and styles depend on the watch face.

Why is my health data blank?+

Open Gym Watch on both iPhone and Apple Watch and allow the Health access you want to use. Check app permissions in Health on iPhone and the health permissions on Watch. Your device must have recorded the relevant samples. Unavailable data or missing access may appear as a dash, rather than an invented number.

What do recent workout time and heart rate mean?+

They refer to the latest available workout record. Average heart rate covers that workout’s time range; it is not a live heart-rate reading. Active energy is today’s total and sleep refers to the latest sleep record. These four metrics do not share the same reporting period.

Why doesn’t a watch complication update immediately?+

Complications display a snapshot cached on your watch. The app requests a refresh after reading Health data or changing settings. watchOS schedules the actual update, and timing also depends on background access, connectivity, and when Health samples are saved. Open the Watch app to check its data, then return to the face. Updates are not guaranteed every second or at a fixed interval.

What if my phone and watch show different data?+

Check pairing, Bluetooth, and connectivity, then open the app on both devices. Verify Health permissions on each device. During a disconnection each device reads its available local data; settings and snapshots are exchanged when connectivity returns. If a difference persists, contact us with the affected metric and observation time.

Does Body Part Energy detect which muscles I trained?+

No. It associates your chosen daily training plan with recorded workout energy or all-day active energy. It does not make Apple Watch identify the body part you actually trained, and all-day active energy is not the energy spent training that body part. Keep your plan updated to reflect what you did.

How do I change language, persona, or fitness mode?+

In iPhone Settings, choose Follow System, Simplified Chinese, or English, and enable Customize Fitness Persona if you want to choose a character. Fitness modes are Build, Cut, Keep, and Chill. Rest in the training plan is a separate day-by-day choice.

Which devices are supported?+

The current version targets iPhone and a paired Apple Watch, with deployment minimums of iOS 17 and watchOS 10. Some visual effects vary by system version. Refer to the App Store listing for final compatibility.

How do I stop Health access?+

You can revoke Gym Watch’s reading permissions in the system’s Health privacy settings. Revoking permission stops future access; it does not necessarily erase snapshots already stored in the app or on a paired device. See the privacy policy for more information.
